Some animals and their characteristics

Bear

The bear is a large, powerful mammal found in forests, mountains, and arctic regions. It is omnivorous, feeding on both plants and animals, and many species hibernate through the winter to conserve energy.

  • Weight: 130 – 360 kg (depending on species)
  • Length: 1.5 – 2.8 meters
  • Main food sources: fish, berries, honey, small mammals, and insects
  • Life expectancy: 20 – 30 years

Lion

Known as the "king of the jungle," the lion actually lives on the savannas of Africa. It is a highly social predator that hunts in groups called prides, usually led by the females.

  • Weight: 120 – 250 kg
  • Length: 1.7 – 2.5 meters (plus tail)
  • Main food sources: zebras, wildebeest, buffalo, and other large herbivores
  • Life expectancy: 10 – 14 years

Shark

Sharks are apex predators of the ocean, present in seas around the world for hundreds of millions of years. Their keen senses of smell and movement detection make them highly efficient hunters.

  • Weight: 60 kg – over 2,000 kg (varies greatly by species)
  • Length: 1.5 – 6 meters (some species larger)
  • Main food sources: fish, seals, other marine mammals, and crustaceans
  • Life expectancy: 20 – 30 years (some species live much longer)
