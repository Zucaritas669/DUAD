BEAR

A bear is a large, heavy mammal with a stocky body, thick fur, short legs, and a keen sense of smell.

Shark

Sharks are a type of cartilaginous fish with skeletons made of flexible cartilage instead of bone.

American Eagle

The American eagle, commonly known as the Bald Eagle, is a large bird of prey with a dark brown body, a striking white head and tail, and a bright yellow hooked bill.
